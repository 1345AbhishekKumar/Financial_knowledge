A stock is an ownership claim on a business. This module covers how markets
price those claims, how they are valued, and how portfolios are built from them.

## What belongs here

- **Instruments** — shares, preferred stock, ETFs, REITs, convertibles
- **Valuation** — P/E, DCF, dividend discount model, book value
- **Analysis** — fundamental vs technical, ratios, statement reading
- **Portfolio theory** — diversification, correlation, risk/return, CAPM
- **Behaviour** — anchoring, loss aversion, herding, market efficiency debate

<Callout type="info">
  Start with market basics before valuation. Without knowing what a share
  represents, the ratios are just numbers.
</Callout>
