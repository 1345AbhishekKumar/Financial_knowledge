## How a notebook is structured

1. **The idea** — one concept, plain language, no jargon without a definition
2. **The example** — real numbers in tables you can follow row by row
3. **The math** — a short snippet that computes the result, so you can change
   the inputs and re-run it

## Writing a notebook

Add a `.mdx` file inside the module folder you want, then list it in that
folder's `meta.json`:

```mdx
---
title: Net present value
description: Discounting future cash flows back to today.
---

## The idea

NPV values an investment by discounting every future cash flow back to today
at a rate that reflects its risk.
```

Your notebook then appears in the sidebar automatically, joins the search
index, and gets its own `llms.mdx` text and OG image.
