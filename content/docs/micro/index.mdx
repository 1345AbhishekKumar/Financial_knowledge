Microeconomics studies the decisions of individuals and small groups: how
consumers allocate a budget, how firms set prices and output, and how those
choices interact in a market.

## What belongs here

- **Foundations** — scarcity, opportunity cost, marginal analysis
- **Curves** — supply, demand, shifts, equilibrium, surplus and shortage
- **Elasticity** — price, income, cross; own vs cross-price effects
- **Market structure** — perfect competition, monopoly, oligopoly, monopolistic competition
- **Consumer & producer theory** — utility, marginal cost, deadweight loss
- **Public policy** — taxes, subsidies, price controls, regulation

<Callout type="info">
  Master supply, demand, and elasticity before anything else. Nearly every
  policy debate in economics is an elasticity debate in disguise.
</Callout>
