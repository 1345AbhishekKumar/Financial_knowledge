Macroeconomics aggregates the decisions of millions of households and firms
into national accounts. It asks why economies grow, why prices rise, and what
policy can and cannot do about it.

## What belongs here

- **National accounts** — GDP, GNP, components, real vs nominal
- **Prices** — CPI, inflation, deflation, cost-push vs demand-pull
- **Labour** — unemployment types, participation, wages, Phillips curve
- **Growth** — Solow model, savings and investment, convergence
- **Stabilisation** — fiscal and monetary policy, transmission lags
- **Open economy** — trade balance, exchange rates, capital flows

<Callout type="info">
  Every aggregate in this module has measurement problems. GDP misses
  unpaid work and the environment; CPI overstates cost-of-living. Read the
  series, not just the number.
</Callout>
